CHATA

The Cabin Near Trenčín

A first-person horror game. Six friends, a wooden cabin in the mountains above Trenčín, way too much borovička, and a drive nobody should have taken.

BASED ON A TRUE STORY.

// january 2020

Three days.
No school. No work.
Just chill.

Thomas, Damian, Christian, Igor, Ema and Veronica rent a wooden cabin in a small settlement above Trenčín for the weekend. A PS5 that won't take the second controller, a toilet disaster nobody will forget, halušky, and Igor's borovička.

Small things are wrong from the start. Footprints in the snow that come from nowhere. A door that opens by itself. Something standing at the edge of the forest.

And at half past two in the morning, Igor has an idea.

"I drive better drunk. I'm more focused."
Igor, 02:34

// the six

Real friends. Real cabin. Real road.

Everyone in CHATA is based on a real person, and the cabin, the settlement and the drive to Medvedice were rebuilt from photos and a video of the real route.

Thomasyou

Nineteen. Holds the phone. Knew it was a stupid idea and went anyway.

Damiancalled the bottom bunk

Glasses, beard, black hoodie. The hot dog was a mistake. His grandpa had rules about the woods.

Christianbest friend

Puts on YouTube nobody watches. Brought the good snacks. Swears there was someone standing between the trees.

Igorthe driver

"Ninety horses of pure power." Opened his first beer at four in the afternoon.

Emathe worried one

"Igor, slow down. That's your fifth." Begged Thomas not to let him drive.

Veronicasledding tomorrow, promise

Wants a photo of everyone before Igor falls asleep in his plate.

// what's inside

Small things. Wrong things.

Your own body

Look down and you see Thomas's hands and shoes. Wash your hands, hold the controller, see yourself in the mirror.

Your phone, any time

TAB: take photos and selfies, read texts from Mom and the group chat... and from an unknown number.

Three people to play

Thomas for most of it, Damian on the toilet, and Christian on the walk into the forest.

Chilla's Art style

Low-res VHS look, slow tension, scares that are never explained and never mentioned again.

A living cabin

Mortal Kombat on a PS5 that rejects your controller, YouTube on the TV, toasts, drunk Igor, everyone asleep at 2 AM.

English + Slovak

Every line in both languages. Na zdravie.
