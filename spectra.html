Graduation day.

You are Scootaloo. Today is the flight test, and everypony is watching. Pass, and you get your wings. Fail… and a kind stallion in a lab coat says there is another way to make you useful.

Board the windowless carriage. Walk the halls of the Weather Factory. Find the six Spectra feathers that tell the truth, hide from the pony who can smell the colours on you, and decide how your story ends.
